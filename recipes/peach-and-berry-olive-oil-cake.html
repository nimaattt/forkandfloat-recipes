Peach and Berry Olive Oil Cake

A soft and moist olive oil cake packed with juicy fruit, finished with a crunchy almond and brown sugar topping.

Prep15 mins
Cook45 mins
Serves8
LevelEasy

Ingredients

  • Mild olive oil90 ml, or 100 g melted butter
  • Sugar80 g
  • Eggs2
  • Plain Greek yogurt170 g
  • Vanilla extract1 tsp
  • All-purpose flour200 g
  • Baking powder1 1/2 tsp
  • Salta pinch
  • Peach1 medium
  • Berries150 g
  • Brown sugar20 g
  • Flaked almonds20 g
  • Cinnamon1/4 tsp

Method

  1. Prepare the batter

    In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, sugar, olive oil, Greek yogurt and vanilla until smooth and well combined.

  2. Add the dry ingredients

    Add the flour, baking powder and a pinch of salt. Fold everything together just until combined, being careful not to overmix.

  3. Add the fruit

    Gently fold the chopped peaches and strawberries as well as the blueberries into the batter until evenly distributed.

  4. Prepare the cake

    Pour the batter into a greased or parchment-lined 20 cm (8-inch) cake pan and spread the top evenly.

  5. Add the topping

    In a small bowl, combine the brown sugar, flaked almonds and cinnamon. Sprinkle evenly over the top of the cake.

  6. Bake

    Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 45–50 minutes, or until golden on top and a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean.

  7. Cool

    Allow the cake to cool in the pan for about 10–15 minutes before removing and slicing.

How to Serve
  • Serve slightly warm on its own.
  • Add a spoonful of whipped cream or a scoop on vanilla ice cream.
  • Serve with fresh berries or stone fruit.
  • Enjoy at room temperature with coffee or tea.
Ava's note

"Use a mild olive oil so it complements rather than overpowers the fruit. You can also replace the olive oil with 100 g melted butter. For a gluten-free version, replace the 200 g all-purpose flour with 150 g almond flour and 50 g coconut flour."
