Persian Saffron Yogurt Chicken (Joojeh Kabab)

A classic Persian saffron yogurt marinade for juicy chicken kabobs.

Prep2 hours
Cook20 mins
Serves2
LevelEasy

Ingredients

  • Chicken breasts2 medium
  • Medium onion1 medium
  • Greek yogurt3/4 cup
  • Bloomed saffron1 tbsp
  • Lemon1/2, juiced
  • Garlic powder1 tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Black pepperto taste

Method

  1. Prepare the ingredients

    Cut the chicken into roughly 2-inch cubes. Slice the onion into thick 1-inch wedges. If making the vegetarian version, replace the chicken with cauliflower florets cut into similar-sized pieces.

  2. Make the marinade

    In a large bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, bloomed saffron, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth and evenly combined.

  3. Marinate

    Add the chicken (or cauliflower) to the marinade. If using chicken, add the onion wedges as well. Mix everything until fully coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight, for the best flavour.

  4. Grill

    Thread the chicken and onions (or cauliflower) onto skewers if desired. Grill over a charcoal BBQ for the most authentic flavour, or use a gas BBQ, turning occasionally until the chicken is fully cooked and lightly charred on the outside. Cauliflower should be tender with nicely caramelized edges.

How to Serve
  • Alongside Persian saffron rice
  • With grilled tomatoes and peppers
  • Wrapped in flatbread with fresh herbs
  • With a side of yogurt and cucumber (Mast-o-Khiar)
Ava's note

"This marinade isn't just for chicken. It's one of my favourite ways to prepare cauliflower for the grill, the yogurt helps create beautiful caramelization while the saffron gives everything its signature golden colour and aroma."
