Persian Cabbage Rolls (Dolmeh Kalam)

Tender cabbage leaves stuffed with a savory mixture of rice, split peas, herbs, and your choice of protein.

Prep30 mins
Cook40 mins
Serves6
LevelMedium

Ingredients

  • Green cabbage1 large
  • Ground beef or soy chunks1 cup
  • Onion or shallot1 small, finely chopped
  • Garlic3 cloves, minced
  • Split peas1/2 cup
  • Persian rice or jasmine rice1 cup
  • Bell pepper1 small or 1/2 large, finely diced
  • Fresh parsley1 cup, finely chopped
  • Olive oil1/2 cup
  • Turmeric powder1 tsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Cumin powder1 tsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Freshly ground black pepperto taste
  • Tomato paste1 cup
  • Simple tomato sauce2 cups

Method

  1. Soak the ingredients

    Place the split peas, rice, and soy chunks, if using, in three separate bowls. Cover each with water and let them soak while you prepare the cabbage and remaining ingredients.

  2. Prepare the cabbage

    Using a sharp knife, carefully remove the hard core from the cabbage. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, the pot should be large enough to fit the whole cabbage. Add the cabbage and simmer for about 10 minutes, or until the leaves have softened and are flexible enough to roll.

  3. Start the filling

    Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until translucent. Add the minced garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.

  4. Add the split peas and tomato paste

    Drain the soaked split peas and add them to the pan. Cook for a couple of minutes. Create an empty space in the center of the pan and add the tomato paste directly to the hot surface. Fry the tomato paste for a couple of minutes to deepen its flavor, then mix it into the onions and split peas.

  5. Add the rice

    Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pan. Pour in 2 cups of water, cover, and simmer for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  6. Cook the beef or soy

    In a separate pan, heat a little olive oil. Drain the soy chunks, if using, or add the ground beef directly to the hot pan. Season with turmeric, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and freshly ground black pepper. Cook for a few minutes until browned and cooked through, then add it to the rice mixture.

  7. Finish the filling

    Add the finely diced bell pepper and chopped parsley. Mix everything together well, then remove from the heat.

  8. Separate the cabbage leaves

    Remove the cabbage from the water and allow it to cool enough to handle. Carefully separate the softened leaves without tearing them. Trim away the thickest part of the center stem from each leaf to make rolling easier.

  9. Fill and roll

    Lay a cabbage leaf flat and spoon some of the filling into the center. Fold the bottom of the leaf over the filling, tuck in both sides, then continue rolling tightly to enclose the filling. Repeat with the remaining cabbage leaves and filling.

  10. Layer the cabbage rolls

    Add a thin layer of tomato sauce to the bottom of the same large pot used for the cabbage. Arrange a layer of cabbage rolls over the sauce, add another layer of tomato sauce, and continue layering until all the rolls are in the pot.

  11. Simmer

    Cover and cook over medium-low heat for about 15 to 20 minutes, allowing the cabbage rolls to absorb the tomato sauce and all the flavors to come together.

How to Serve
  • Serve hot with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.
  • Finish with fresh parsley and freshly ground black pepper.
  • Enjoy cold or at room temperature as well.
  • Serve on its own or alongside yogurt and fresh herbs.
Ava's note

"For a completely vegan version, use soy chunks instead of ground beef. These cabbage rolls are especially good for meal prep because the flavors continue to develop as they sit. Store them in the refrigerator and reheat as needed, or freeze them in an airtight container for up to 2 months."
