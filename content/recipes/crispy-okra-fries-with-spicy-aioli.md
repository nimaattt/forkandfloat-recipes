ingredients:
  - name: "Okra"
    amount: "250 g"
  - name: "All-purpose flour"
    amount: "1/4 cup"
  - name: "Cornstarch"
    amount: "1/4 cup"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Smoked paprika"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Egg"
    amount: "1"
  - name: "Panko breadcrumbs"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Neutral oil spray"
    amount: "as needed"
  - name: "Mayonnaise"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Dijon mustard"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Tabasco"
    amount: "2 tsp, or to taste"
  - name: "Rice vinegar"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 1/2 tsp"
  - name: "Chives"
    amount: "for garnish, chopped"
steps:
  - title: "Prepare the coating stations"
    body: "In the first bowl, combine the flour, cornstarch, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika. In the second bowl, whisk together the egg with a pinch of salt and pepper until fully combined. Add the panko breadcrumbs to a third bowl."
  - title: "Prepare the okra"
    body: "Wash the okra and dry it thoroughly. Cut each okra lengthwise in half."
  - title: "Bread the okra"
    body: "Coat each piece of okra in the flour mixture, then dip into the egg mixture, and finally coat with the panko breadcrumbs. Repeat until all the okra is breaded."
  - title: "Air fry the okra"
    body: "Line an air fryer basket with a perforated parchment paper or an air fryer liner that allows airflow. Arrange the okra in a single layer and spray generously with neutral oil."
  - title: "Cook at 420°F for about 10 minutes, then flip the okra, spray again if needed, and cook for another 10 minutes or until golden brown and crispy."
    body: ""
  - title: "Make the aioli"
    body: "In a small bowl, combine the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, Tabasco, rice vinegar, and olive oil. Mix until smooth. Top with chopped chives."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Serve the crispy okra fries immediately with the spicy Tabasco aioli on the side."
howToServe:
  - "Serve as a snack or appetizer."
  - "Pair with burgers, sandwiches, or BBQ dishes."
  - "Serve with extra Tabasco on the side for additional heat."
  - "Enjoy fresh from the air fryer while still crispy."
chefsNote: "Drying the okra thoroughly before breading is key to achieving a crispy coating. 
Avoid overcrowding the air fryer basket and cook in batches if needed. 
The aioli can be made a day ahead and stored in the refrigerator until ready to serve."
