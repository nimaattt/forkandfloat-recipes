tags:
  - "vegetarian"

prepTime: "5 mins"
cookTime: "10 mins"

date: 2026-08-05T21:26:35.364Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Green beans"
    amount: "3 cups"
  - name: "Greek yogurt"
    amount: "1 1/2 cups"
  - name: "Fresh cilantro"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Fresh parsley"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Green onions"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Lemon"
    amount: "1, juice + zest"
  - name: "Extra virgin olive oil"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Sugar snap peas"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "optional"
  - name: "Fresh mint"
    amount: "few leaves"
  - name: "Fresh dill"
    amount: "few leaves"
steps:
  - title: "Make the dip"
    body: "Add the green beans, Greek yogurt, cilantro, parsley, green onions, olive oil, lemon juice and zest, salt, pepper, and garlic, if using, to a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth and creamy."
  - title: "Char the snap peas"
    body: "Lightly coat the sugar snap peas with olive oil. Char them on a hot grill or pan until blistered and browned in spots while still keeping a little crunch. Remove from the heat and allow to cool slightly."
  - title: "Make the topping"
    body: "Toss the charred snap peas with fresh mint, parsley, and dill, keeping the herbs in larger pieces rather than finely chopping them. Add a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt and gently toss."
  - title: "Plate"
    body: "Spread the green bean dip onto a serving plate or shallow bowl. Spoon the charred snap pea and herb mixture generously over the top and finish with a little extra olive oil."
howToServe:
  - "Serve as an appetizer with crackers, toasted bread, or fresh vegetables."
chefsNote: "Don't overcook the sugar snap peas, you want them deeply charred in spots but still crisp. 
Keeping the garnish herbs in larger pieces also gives the topping more texture and makes the dip feel almost like a little salad on top."
