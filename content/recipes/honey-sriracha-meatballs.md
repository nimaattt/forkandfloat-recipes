dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Ground beef"
    amount: "500 g"
  - name: "Shallot or onion"
    amount: "1 small"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "2 cloves"
  - name: "Fresh parsley"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Breadcrumbs"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Milk"
    amount: "1/2 cup, any kind"
  - name: "Paprika"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp, plus more as needed for cooking"
  - name: "Honey"
    amount: "2/3 cup"
  - name: "Sriracha sauce"
    amount: "2/3 cup"
  - name: "Lemon"
    amount: "1 small"
  - name: "Cornstarch"
    amount: "1 tsp"
steps:
  - title: "Prepare the meatball mixture"
    body: "Grate the shallot and mince the garlic. Add them to a large bowl with the ground beef, parsley, breadcrumbs, milk, paprika, salt, and freshly ground black pepper. Mix well until everything is fully incorporated and the mixture becomes slightly sticky."
  - title: "Rest the mixture"
    body: "Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. This gives the mixture time to firm up and the flavors to come together."
  - title: "Shape the meatballs"
    body: "Remove the mixture from the refrigerator and mix it again. Take portions of the mixture and gently roll them between your palms to form meatballs in your desired size."
  - title: "Cook the meatballs"
    body: "Heat the olive oil in a medium pan over medium heat. Add the meatballs and cook, turning gently as needed, until golden on all sides and completely cooked through. Be careful when turning them so they hold their shape. Remove from the pan and set aside."
  - title: "Make the honey sriracha glaze"
    body: "In a separate pan, heat a small drizzle of olive oil over medium heat. Add the honey, sriracha, and juice of half the lemon. Stir until smooth and well combined."
  - title: "Thicken the glaze"
    body: "In a small cup, mix the cornstarch with 1/3 cup cold water until completely smooth. Pour it into the honey sriracha mixture and stir well. Cook over medium heat for about 5 minutes, or until the sauce has slightly thickened and become glossy."
  - title: "Glaze the meatballs"
    body: "Add the cooked meatballs to the sauce and gently toss until completely coated. Let them warm together for a couple of minutes before serving."
howToServe:
  - "Serve as an appetizer with toothpicks."
  - "Serve over steamed rice for a main dish."
  - "Pair with a crunchy slaw or fresh cucumber salad."
  - "Finish with sliced green onions or sesame seeds if desired."
chefsNote: "These are great for prepping ahead. Make the meatballs and glaze separately, refrigerate them, then combine and gently reheat everything together in a pan just before serving. Keeping them separate until reheating helps the meatballs maintain their texture."
