steps:
  - title: "Blanch the basil"
    body: ""
  - title: "Bring a pot of water to a boil over high heat. Add the basil leaves and blanch for about 1 minute. Immediately transfer the basil to an ice bath to stop the cooking and preserve the bright green colour."
    body: ""
  - title: "Drain and dry"
    body: ""
  - title: "Once cooled, drain the basil and gently squeeze out as much excess water as possible."
    body: ""
  - title: "Blend the oil"
    body: ""
  - title: "Add the blanched basil, grapeseed oil, and olive oil to a blender. Blend for 3–4 minutes until completely smooth and the oil turns a vibrant green."
    body: ""
  - title: "Strain the oil"
    body: ""
  - title: "Pour the mixture through a fine-mesh strainer. Strain a second time through a cheesecloth into a clean container. Allow the oil to strain naturally without pressing or forcing the liquid through."
    body: ""
  - title: "Store"
    body: ""
  - title: "Transfer the strained oil to a clean jar or bottle and keep refrigerated until ready to use."
    body: ""
howToServe:
  - "Drizzle over lemon pasta."

  - "Use as a garnish for soups."
  - "Spoon over burrata or fresh cheese boards."
