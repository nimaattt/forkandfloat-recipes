ingredients:
  - name: "Full-fat coconut milk"
    amount: "1 can"
  - name: "Gelatin powder"
    amount: "2 tsp"
  - name: "Water"
    amount: "1/2 cup, for blooming gelatin"
  - name: "Honey or maple syrup"
    amount: "1 1/2 tbsp"
  - name: "Vanilla extract"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Mixed berries"
    amount: "1 1/2 cups"
  - name: "Maple syrup"
    amount: "1 tbsp, for berry compote"
  - name: "Gelatin powder"
    amount: "1 tsp, for berry compote"
  - name: "Water"
    amount: "1 tbsp, for blooming gelatin"
  - name: "Salted pistachios"
    amount: "7–8, roughly crushed"
steps:
  - title: "Bloom the gelatin"
    body: ""
  - title: "In a small bowl, mix 2 tsp gelatin powder with 1/2 cup water. Let it sit for a few minutes until fully bloomed and slightly thickened."
    body: ""
  - title: "Make the panna cotta"
    body: ""
  - title: "Pour the coconut milk into a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir gently while it warms up. Add the honey or maple syrup and vanilla extract. Once the mixture is warm, stir in the bloomed gelatin until fully dissolved. Remove from the heat."
    body: ""
  - title: "Chill the panna cotta"
    body: ""
  - title: "Pour the mixture into one large serving bowl or four individual serving dishes. Transfer to the fridge and chill for at least 4 hours, or until completely set."
    body: ""
  - title: "Make the berry compote"
    body: ""
  - title: "Bloom 1 tsp gelatin powder in 2 tbsp water and set aside. Add the mixed berries and maple syrup to a small saucepan and simmer for about 10 minutes until the berries begin to break down and release their juices. Stir in the bloomed gelatin until dissolved. Remove from the heat and refrigerate until cooled."
    body: ""
  - title: "Assemble and serve"
    body: ""
  - title: "Spoon the cooled berry compote over the set panna cotta. Top with crushed salted pistachios and serve chilled."
    body: ""
howToServe:
  - "Serve chilled as a light dessert."
  - "Top with additional fresh berries."
  - "Great for dinner parties and special occasions."
chefsNote: "This is one of my favourite make-ahead desserts. 
The coconut milk keeps it light while still feeling rich and creamy, and the tart berries balance everything beautifully. 
For a sweeter version, you can increase the maple syrup or substitute it with sugar. 
The panna cotta and berry compote can be made up to 3 days ahead and kept refrigerated until ready to serve."
