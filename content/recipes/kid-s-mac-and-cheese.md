emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Macaroni"
    amount: "500 g"
  - name: "Béchamel sauce"
    amount: "500 g"
  - name: "Cheddar cheese"
    amount: "100 g, grated"
  - name: "Mozzarella"
    amount: "100 g, grated"
  - name: "Parmesan"
    amount: "50 g, grated"
  - name: "Grana Padano"
    amount: "50 g, grated"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "a drizzle"
  - name: "Breadcrumbs"
    amount: "for topping"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste, optional"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the macaroni"
    body: "Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the macaroni and cook until al dente. Drain well, then toss with a small drizzle of olive oil."
  - title: "Assemble the mac and cheese"
    body: "Transfer the macaroni to a baking dish. Add the béchamel sauce, cheddar and mozzarella. Season with salt and pepper, if desired, and mix well until the pasta is evenly coated."
  - title: "Add the topping"
    body: "Flatten the top into an even layer. Sprinkle with the Parmesan, Grana Padano, and a light layer of breadcrumbs and the garlic powder."
  - title: "Bake"
    body: "Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and make a few small holes in the top. Bake at 400°F for about 20 minutes, until the cheese is completely melted and everything is hot and bubbly."
  - title: "Brown the top"
    body: "If the top isn't golden enough, remove the foil and bake uncovered at 400°F for another 5 minutes, or until golden brown and lightly crispy."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Let the mac and cheese rest for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy warm while it's creamy and cheesy."
howToServe:
  - "Serve on its own as a kid-friendly main."
  - "Pair with steamed or roasted vegetables."
  - "Serve as a side for BBQs or family dinners."
chefsNote: "The béchamel sauce recipe can be found in the recipe hub. 
This mac and cheese can also be assembled ahead of time, covered, and kept in the refrigerator until you're ready to bake it. If baking straight from the fridge, it may need a few extra minutes in the oven."
