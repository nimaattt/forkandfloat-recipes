description: "A creamy, cheesy baked dip loaded with spinach, artichokes, and garlic, with a golden mozzarella topping."
category: "Sides"
tags:
  - "vegetarian"
  - "crowd"
  - "sides"
prepTime: "10 mins"
cookTime: "20 mins"
servings: "3-4"
difficulty: "Easy"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/19ykabqiyS-77n8qNnU2UF9YhXRiAbOOP"
date: 2026-07-03T13:38:33.536Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Spinach"
    amount: "3 cups, raw"
  - name: "Artichoke hearts"
    amount: "200 g or 1 can, drained"
  - name: "Cream cheese"
    amount: "250 g"
  - name: "Mozzarella"
    amount: "1 cup, grated"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "4–5 cloves"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the garlic"
    body: "Heat the olive oil in a medium pan over medium heat. Mince the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - title: "Cook the spinach"
    body: "Chop the spinach roughly and add to the garlic, and cook for a few minutes until wilted and most of the moisture has evaporated."
  - title: "Add the artichokes"
    body: "Cut the artichoke hearts into small pieces and add them to the pan. Cook for another 2–3 minutes."
  - title: "Make the dip mixture"
    body: "Add the cream cheese and stir until fully melted and combined. Add half of the mozzarella, salt, black pepper, and garlic powder. Mix well until creamy and smooth."
  - title: "Prepare for baking"
    body: "Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Transfer the mixture to an oven-safe serving dish and smooth the top."
  - title: "Bake"
    body: "Sprinkle the remaining mozzarella evenly over the top. Broil for about 10 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and lightly golden."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Remove from the oven and let cool for a few minutes before serving."
howToServe:
  - "Serve warm with crackers."
  - "Serve with toasted baguette slices."
  - "Pair with fresh vegetables such as carrots, celery, or cucumbers."
  - "Add to a grazing table or appetizer spread."
chefsNote: "Make sure to keep an eye on it while in the oven as the cheese can brown quickly. 
Leftovers can be stored in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheated in the oven or microwave."
