steps:
  - title: "Mix the batter"
    body: "Transfer the herbs to a large bowl. Add the eggs, turmeric, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, barberries and walnuts. Mix well until everything is evenly combined."
  - title: "Cook the first side"
    body: "Heat a generous amount of oil in a non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Add the herb mixture and use a spatula to flatten the top evenly. Cover with a lid and cook for about 15 minutes, until the bottom is golden and set."
  - title: "Flip and finish cooking"
    body: "Carefully flip the kookoo sabzi and cook the other side uncovered for another 10–15 minutes, until fully cooked through and crispy on the outside."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Let cool slightly before slicing and serving."
