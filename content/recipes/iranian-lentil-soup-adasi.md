description: "A warm and cozy lentil soup with potatoes, mushrooms and lemon for the colder months."
category: "Mains"
tags:
  - "mains"
  - "vegetarian"
  - "persian"
prepTime: "10 minutes"
cookTime: "55–65 minutes"
servings: "4"
difficulty: "Easy"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1CDK1ed_Vcd2j4LRv4UKCuWVwQc03L2oq"
date: 2026-10-15T20:16:32.858Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Green lentils"
    amount: "3 cups"
  - name: "Potatoes"
    amount: "2 medium"
  - name: "Mushrooms (porcini or white)"
    amount: "10–12 medium"
  - name: "Lemons"
    amount: "2"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "½ cup"
  - name: "Chilli flakes"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Fresh cilantro"
    amount: "½ cup"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste, optional"
steps:
  - title: "Start the lentils"
    body: "Rinse the lentils and place them in a large pot with about 4 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium and cook for about 10 minutes."
  - title: "Add the potatoes"
    body: "Peel the potatoes and cut them into small cubes of your preferred size. Add them to the lentils, cover and simmer for at least 30 minutes, until both the lentils and potatoes are tender. Stir occasionally and add more water as needed to keep everything cooking comfortably and reach your preferred soup consistency."
  - title: "Add the olive oil, mushrooms and lemon"
    body: "Stir in the olive oil. Slice the mushrooms and add them to the pot, immediately followed by the juice of both lemons. Cover and simmer for about 15 minutes, until the mushrooms are cooked through and the flavours have come together."
  - title: "Season and finish"
    body: "Add the salt and chilli flakes, along with black pepper if desired. Finely chop the cilantro and stir half into the soup just before serving. Reserve the rest for garnish."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Ladle into bowls and finish with the remaining cilantro, extra chilli flakes and a drizzle of olive oil. Enjoy hot."
howToServe:
  - "Serve hot as part of a brunch spread."
  - "Enjoy as a full meal with bread and a salad on the side."
chefsNote: "Add the salt at the end and adjust it to your taste. Make sure the lentils are tender before adding the lemon juice. I like to finish each bowl with a little more olive oil, chilli flakes and fresh cilantro."
