prepTime: "Approximately 20 minutes, plus soaking"
cookTime: "Approximately 50 minutes"
servings: "4"
difficulty: "Medium"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1VHgSOy4YAIdIesVn4V7bJ9ESgoW7ejR5"

emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Large bell pepper"
    amount: "4, yellow, orange or red"
  - name: "Soy chunks"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Onion or shallot"
    amount: "1 small, finely chopped"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "3 cloves, minced"
  - name: "Split peas"
    amount: "½ cup"
  - name: "Persian rice or jasmine rice"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Bell pepper"
    amount: "1 small or ½ large, finely diced"
  - name: "Fresh parsley"
    amount: "1 cup, finely chopped"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "½ cup"
  - name: "Turmeric powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Smoked paprika"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Cumin powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Tomato paste"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Simple tomato sauce"
    amount: "2 cups"
steps:
  - title: "Soak the ingredients"
    body: "Place the split peas, rice and soy chunks in three separate bowls. Cover each with water and let them soak while you prepare the bell peppers and remaining ingredients."
  - title: "Prepare the bell peppers"
    body: "Cut around the top of each large bell pepper, leaving some of the pepper flesh attached to the stem to create a lid. Set the lids aside. Remove the seeds and white membranes from inside the peppers, keeping the peppers intact."
  - title: "Start the filling"
    body: "Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until translucent. Add the minced garlic and cook briefly until fragrant."
  - title: "Add the split peas and tomato paste"
    body: "Drain the soaked split peas and add them to the pan. Cook for a couple of minutes. Create an empty space in the centre of the pan and add the tomato paste directly to the hot surface. Fry the tomato paste for a couple of minutes to deepen its flavour, then mix it into the onions and split peas."
  - title: "Add the rice"
    body: "Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pan. Pour in 2 cups of water, cover and simmer for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally."
  - title: "Cook the soy chunks"
    body: "In a separate pan, heat a little olive oil. Drain the soaked soy chunks and add them to the hot pan. Season with turmeric, smoked paprika, cumin, salt and freshly ground black pepper. Cook for a few minutes until browned and cooked through, then add them to the rice mixture."
  - title: "Finish the filling"
    body: "Add the finely diced bell pepper and chopped parsley. Mix everything together well, then remove from the heat."
  - title: "Stuff the bell peppers"
    body: "Spoon the filling into the prepared bell peppers, then place the pepper lids back on top."
  - title: "Add the tomato sauce"
    body: "Spread some of the tomato sauce over the bottom of a pan. Place the stuffed bell peppers upright on top, then spoon more tomato sauce over and around them."
  - title: "Cook until tender"
    body: "Cover and cook over medium heat for about 30 minutes, or until the bell peppers are fully cooked through and tender."
howToServe:
  - "Serve hot with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil."
  - "Finish with fresh parsley and freshly ground black pepper."
  - "Serve on its own or alongside yogurt and fresh herbs."
chefsNote: "Keep some of the pepper flesh attached to the stem when cutting off the top—it becomes the lid once the pepper is filled. You can use yellow, orange or red bell peppers."
