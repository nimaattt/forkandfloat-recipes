emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Seaweed sheets"
    amount: "3 large or 12 small"
  - name: "Rice paper"
    amount: "2–3 large sheets"
  - name: "Sesame seeds"
    amount: "for sprinkling"
steps:
  - title: "Prepare the wrappers"
    body: "Cut the rice paper and seaweed sheets so they are roughly the same size."
  - title: "Assemble"
    body: "Lay the seaweed sheets flat and sprinkle with sesame seeds."
  - title: "Quickly dip one rice paper sheet into warm water, just long enough to soften slightly, then immediately remove it and place it directly on top of the seaweed. The rice paper will continue softening as it sits."
    body: ""
  - title: "Shape the cups"
    body: "Once softened, gently press each seaweed-rice paper piece into a small microwave-safe bowl or cup to create a cup shape."
  - title: "Cook"
    body: "Microwave for about 2 minutes, or until completely dry and crispy. Cooking time may vary slightly depending on your microwave."
  - title: "Cool"
    body: "Allow the cups to cool for a few minutes before removing them from the bowls. They will continue to crisp up as they cool."
  - title: "Fill and serve"
    body: "Fill the crispy cups with your favourite fillings and serve immediately."
howToServe:
  - "Fill with spicy salmon and avocado."
  - "Fill with marinated tofu and crunchy vegetables."
  - "Serve with poke-style fillings."
  - "Fill with fresh fruit and yogurt for a sweet version."
  - "Top with herbs, sesame seeds, or a drizzle of spicy mayo."
chefsNote: "These cups are incredibly versatile and can be filled with almost anything. 
Try them with sushi-inspired fillings, chopped salads, grilled vegetables, or even fruit for a fun dessert. 
For the best texture, fill them just before serving so they stay crisp."
