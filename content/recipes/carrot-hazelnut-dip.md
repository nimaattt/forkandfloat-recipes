emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Carrots"
    amount: "3 large"
  - name: "Roasted hazelnuts"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Cooked chickpeas"
    amount: "1 cup, canned or cooked"
  - name: "Extra virgin olive oil"
    amount: "½ cup, plus extra for roasting"
  - name: "Ground cinnamon"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Ground nutmeg"
    amount: "½ tsp"
  - name: "Lemon"
    amount: "½, juiced"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Roast the carrots"
    body: "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice the carrots and toss them with a drizzle of olive oil, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt and black pepper. Spread onto a baking tray and roast for about 15 minutes, until tender and lightly golden around the edges. Let the carrots cool slightly."
  - title: "Blend the dip"
    body: "Add the roasted carrots, chickpeas, roasted hazelnuts, lemon juice, olive oil and a pinch more salt to a blender. Blend until completely smooth and creamy. Taste and adjust with more salt, lemon juice or cinnamon if desired."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Transfer to a serving bowl, drizzle with olive oil and serve with crackers, toasted sourdough or fresh vegetables."
howToServe:
  - "With toasted sourdough"
  - "With crackers or pita chips"
  - "Alongside grilled vegetables"
  - "As part of a grazing board"
chefsNote: "Roasting the carrots brings out their natural sweetness, so don't rush this step. 
If the dip feels too thick, blend in a tablespoon or two of cold water until you reach your preferred consistency."
