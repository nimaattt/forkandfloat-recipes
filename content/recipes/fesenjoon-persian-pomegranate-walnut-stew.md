title: "Fesenjoon (Persian Pomegranate & Walnut Stew)"
description: "A rich and deeply comforting Persian stew."
category: "Mains"
tags:
  - "mains"
  - "vegetarian"
  - "persian"
prepTime: "5 mins"
cookTime: "4 hours"
servings: "3-4"
difficulty: "Medium"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1HqOJsi3lwS6Z823N9WxNcoc1LgX9oCkr"
date: 2026-06-05T15:39:13.303Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Raw walnut"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Ice cubes or ice-cold water"
    amount: "About 10 cubes"
  - name: "Pomegranate molasses"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Sugar"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Mushrooms"
    amount: "250 g"
  - name: "Butter or oil for frying mushrooms"
    amount: "1/2 tbsp"
  - name: "Lemon or lime juice (optional)"
    amount: "1/2 tbsp"
  - name: "Salt & pepper"
    amount: "To taste"
steps:
  - title: "Using a blender or food processor, grind the walnuts until they reach a paste-like consistency."
    body: ""
  - title: "Add the walnut paste to a hot pot without any oil. Cook over medium heat for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until the raw walnut smell disappears."
    body: ""
  - title: "Add enough cold water to fully cover the walnuts. Cover with a lid and cook for about 1 hour, stirring occasionally."
    body: ""
  - title: "Add about 5 ice cubes and continue cooking for another hour. Repeat this process one more time with additional ice cubes until the stew darkens in color and thickens in texture."
    body: ""
  - title: "Stir in the pomegranate molasses and sugar. Cover again and cook for another 30 minutes."
    body: ""
  - title: "In a separate pan, fry the mushrooms with butter or oil, salt, pepper, and a small squeeze of lemon or lime juice if using."
    body: ""
  - title: "Once the mushrooms are fully cooked, add them to the walnut mixture and let everything simmer together for a few more minutes so the flavors absorb."
    body: ""
howToServe:
  - "Serve hot with Persian rice and fresh herbs on the side."
chefsNote: "If you are not vegetarian or vegan, you can replace the mushrooms with meatballs or chicken. Cook them separately and add them to the stew toward the end.
The longer Fesenjoon cooks, the deeper and richer the flavor becomes.
Leftovers taste even better the next day.
If you like your stew on the sweeter side, add more sugar.
Do not add any oil. Walnuts will release oil as they cook."
