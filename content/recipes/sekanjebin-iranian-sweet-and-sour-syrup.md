emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Water"
    amount: "3 cups"
  - name: "Sugar"
    amount: "3 cups"
  - name: "White vinegar"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Fresh mint"
    amount: "1 cup"
steps:
  - title: "Make the syrup"
    body: "In a medium pot, bring the water to a boil."
  - title: "Dissolve the sugar"
    body: "Add the sugar and boil over medium-high heat for about 15 minutes, or until the syrup has slightly thickened."
  - title: "Add the vinegar"
    body: "Pour in the vinegar and continue cooking over medium heat for another 10 minutes."
  - title: "Infuse the mint"
    body: "Add the fresh mint and turn off the heat. Let the mint steep in the syrup for about 10 minutes."
  - title: "Strain and chill"
    body: "Strain out the mint leaves and transfer the syrup to a container. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before using."
howToServe:
  - "For a traditional Sekanjebin drink, add 2 tbsp of syrup to a large glass."
  - "Grate 1 cucumber directly into the glass."
  - "Fill the glass with ice."
  - "Top with cold water and stir well."
  - "Serve immediately."
chefsNote: "Sekanjebin is one of Iran's most beloved summer refreshments. The balance of sweet sugar, tangy vinegar, and cooling mint creates a surprisingly refreshing syrup. 
Store the syrup in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 months. 
Adjust the amount of syrup to taste depending on how sweet or tart you like your drink."
