emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Halloumi cheese"
    amount: "1 block, about 200 g"
  - name: "Watermelon"
    amount: "⅓ cup, finely diced"
  - name: "Melon of choice"
    amount: "⅓ cup, finely diced"
  - name: "Hot honey"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Fresh cilantro"
    amount: "⅓ cup, finely chopped"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Flaky salt"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Lime"
    amount: "1"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Prepare the halloumi"
    body: "Cut the halloumi into your desired size pieces and thread onto wooden skewers. Drizzle with olive oil and season with freshly ground black pepper."
  - title: "Make the melon relish"
    body: "Cut the watermelon and melon into the smallest cubes possible. Add them to a bowl with the zest of one lime and the juice of half the lime. Mix gently and let it sit while the halloumi cooks."
  - title: "Grill the halloumi"
    body: "Preheat the BBQ over medium-high heat. Once hot, place the skewers on the grill and cook, turning frequently, until all four sides have grill marks and the halloumi is golden and crispy on the outside."
  - title: "Finish and serve"
    body: "Transfer the grilled halloumi skewers to a serving plate. Drizzle generously with hot honey, spoon the melon relish over top, and finish with flaky salt and chopped cilantro."
howToServe:
  - "As a summer appetizer"
  - "Alongside grilled vegetables"
  - "As part of a BBQ spread"
  - "On it's own as a vegetarian main"
chefsNote: "The key to this dish is cutting the melon into very small cubes so it sits beautifully on the skewers. 
Serve immediately while the halloumi is still warm and crispy. 
The contrast of salty cheese, sweet melon and spicy honey is what makes this dish special."
