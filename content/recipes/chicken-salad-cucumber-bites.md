cookTime: "1 hour"
servings: "5-6"
difficulty: "Easy"

date: 2026-08-25T15:46:00.758Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Chicken breasts"
    amount: "2"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "2 cloves, whole"
  - name: "Pepperoncini"
    amount: "5–6 large pieces"
  - name: "Gala apple"
    amount: "1 medium"
  - name: "Celery"
    amount: "3 large stalks"
  - name: "Dijon mustard"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Mayonnaise"
    amount: "3 tbsp"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp, plus extra for cooking"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste, if needed"
  - name: "English cucumbers"
    amount: "optional, 3 medium"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the chicken"
    body: "Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a medium pot. Add the whole garlic cloves and chicken breasts. Sear the chicken for about 4 minutes on each side, then add enough hot water to partially cover the chicken. Cook over medium heat for about 1 hour, or until the chicken is completely cooked through."
  - title: "Shred the chicken"
    body: "Remove the chicken from the pot and let it cool completely. Using your hands, two forks, or a food processor, shred the chicken to your desired texture."
  - title: "Prepare the salad"
    body: "Finely chop the celery and pepperoncini. Thinly slice and chop the apple. Add everything to a large bowl with the shredded chicken."
  - title: "Mix the dressing"
    body: "Add the Dijon mustard, mayonnaise, olive oil, and freshly ground black pepper. Mix well until everything is evenly coated. Taste before adding any salt—the pepperoncini are already quite salty, so you may not need much."
  - title: "Chill"
    body: "Refrigerate the chicken salad until you're ready to serve. Chilling also gives the flavors time to come together."
  - title: "Assemble and serve"
    body: "Cut the cucumbers into thick rounds or large bite-sized pieces and spoon the chilled chicken salad on top. Assemble just before serving to keep the cucumber crisp."
howToServe:
  - "Serve on thick cucumber pieces for a fresh appetizer."
  - "Spoon onto crackers for an easy snack."
  - "Use as a filling for sandwiches or wraps."
  - "Serve on its own as a make-ahead lunch."
chefsNote: "The combination of sweet apple, crunchy celery, and tangy pepperoncini keeps this chicken salad fresh rather than overly creamy. 
If you're serving it on cucumber, assemble the bites just before serving so the cucumber stays crisp and doesn't release too much water."
