description: "Creamy and garlicky shrimp filling layered between soft white bread and finished with a fresh chive edge."
category: "Snacks"
tags:
  - "crowd"
  - "sides"
prepTime: "15 mins"
cookTime: "5 mins"
servings: "6"
difficulty: "Easy"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1VPPx6oOQGD4lL1AFMkpd7fnbW9PTmdJp"
date: 2026-08-31T22:51:42.402Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Raw shrimp"
    amount: "500 g, cleaned and tails removed"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "3 cloves, minced"
  - name: "Paprika"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "for cooking"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Dijon mustard"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Kewpie mayonnaise"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Whipped cream cheese"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "White sandwich bread"
    amount: "as needed"
  - name: "Fresh chives"
    amount: "1 bunch, finely chopped"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the shrimp"
    body: "Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a medium pan over high heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for less than 1 minute, just until fragrant. Add the shrimp and cook for about 1 minute per side, or just until cooked through. Season with paprika, salt, and freshly ground black pepper. Be careful not to overcook the shrimp or they will become chewy."
  - title: "Make the shrimp filling"
    body: "Remove the shrimp from the heat and allow them to cool slightly. Finely chop the cooked shrimp, then add the Dijon mustard and Kewpie mayonnaise. Mix until well combined."
  - title: "Prepare the chives"
    body: "Finely slice the chives and spread them evenly onto a flat plate."
  - title: "Assemble the sandwiches"
    body: "Spread a generous layer of the shrimp filling onto a slice of white bread and top with another slice. Trim off the crusts so you're left with just the soft center of the bread."
  - title: "Cut the sandwiches"
    body: "Using a sharp bread knife, cut each sandwich cleanly in half."
  - title: "Finish with chives"
    body: "Spread a thin layer of whipped cream cheese along one cut edge of each sandwich. Gently press that edge into the chopped chives until completely coated."
howToServe:
  - "Serve as passed bites or appetizers."
  - "Add to an afternoon tea spread."
  - "Serve as part of a brunch or grazing table."
  - "Enjoy chilled or at room temperature."
chefsNote: "The shrimp only need a couple of minutes to cook, so keep a close eye on them to make sure they stay tender and juicy. 
If you're preparing these ahead of time, make the shrimp filling and keep it refrigerated, then assemble and garnish the sandwiches closer to serving so the bread stays fresh and soft."
