dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Beetroots"
    amount: "3 medium, peeled and sliced"
  - name: "Ricotta cheese"
    amount: "1 1/2 cups"
  - name: "Lemon zest"
    amount: "from 1/2 lemon"
  - name: "Lemon juice"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Cumin powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Honey"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Hot honey"
    amount: "1 tbsp, optional"
  - name: "Extra virgin olive oil"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Beetroot leaves or arugula"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Crushed pistachios"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Roast the beetroot"
    body: "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the sliced beetroot with 1 tbsp olive oil, cumin powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for about 20 minutes until tender, browned, and slightly charred around the edges."
  - title: "Prepare the ricotta"
    body: "In a bowl, mix the ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice, honey, and a pinch of salt until smooth and creamy."
  - title: "Assemble the salad"
    body: "Spread the ricotta mixture onto a serving plate. Finish with a few grinds of black pepper. Arrange the roasted beetroot on top, then drizzle with hot honey if using."
  - title: "Finish and serve"
    body: "Scatter the beetroot leaves or arugula over the salad. Sprinkle with crushed pistachios and finish with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil before serving."
howToServe:
  - "As a starter for a dinner party"
  - "Alongside grilled vegetables or BBQ"
  - "With fresh sourdough bread"
  - "As part of a mezze-style spread"
chefsNote: "The contrast between the sweet earthy beetroot, creamy ricotta, crunchy pistachios, and spicy hot honey is what makes this dish special. 
If your beetroot comes with fresh leaves attached, don't throw them away, they make the perfect garnish."
