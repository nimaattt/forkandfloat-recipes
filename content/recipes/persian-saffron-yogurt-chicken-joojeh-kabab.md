emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Chicken breasts"
    amount: "2 medium"
  - name: "Medium onion"
    amount: "1 medium"
  - name: "Greek yogurt"
    amount: "3/4 cup"
  - name: "Bloomed saffron"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Lemon"
    amount: "1/2, juiced"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Prepare the ingredients"
    body: "Cut the chicken into roughly 2-inch cubes. Slice the onion into thick 1-inch wedges. If making the vegetarian version, replace the chicken with cauliflower florets cut into similar-sized pieces."
  - title: "Make the marinade"
    body: "In a large bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, bloomed saffron, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth and evenly combined."
  - title: "Marinate"
    body: "Add the chicken (or cauliflower) to the marinade. If using chicken, add the onion wedges as well. Mix everything until fully coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight, for the best flavour."
  - title: "Grill"
    body: "Thread the chicken and onions (or cauliflower) onto skewers if desired. Grill over a charcoal BBQ for the most authentic flavour, or use a gas BBQ, turning occasionally until the chicken is fully cooked and lightly charred on the outside. Cauliflower should be tender with nicely caramelized edges."
howToServe:
  - "Alongside Persian saffron rice"
  - "With grilled tomatoes and peppers"
  - "Wrapped in flatbread with fresh herbs"
  - "With a side of yogurt and cucumber (Mast-o-Khiar)"
chefsNote: "This marinade isn't just for chicken. It's one of my favourite ways to prepare cauliflower for the grill, the yogurt helps create beautiful caramelization while the saffron gives everything its signature golden colour and aroma."
