category: "Mains"

  - "vegetarian"
prepTime: "5 mins"
cookTime: "15 mins"
servings: "5-6"
difficulty: "Easy"

date: 2026-08-11T21:34:19.145Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Eggs"
    amount: "12 large"
  - name: "Milk"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Butter"
    amount: "50 g"
  - name: "Fresh chives"
    amount: "1/2 cup, finely chopped"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Whisk the eggs"
    body: "Crack the eggs into a large bowl and add the milk. Whisk well for 2–3 minutes, until completely combined and slightly airy."
  - title: "Melt the butter"
    body: "In a medium pan, melt the butter over medium-low heat."
  - title: "Cook the eggs"
    body: "Pour in the egg mixture. Using a spatula, stir constantly, making sure the eggs don't stick to the bottom of the pan. Continue cooking slowly for about 10–15 minutes."
  - title: "Season"
    body: "Once the eggs begin to thicken and form larger, soft curds, add the salt and freshly ground black pepper. Continue stirring until they reach your desired consistency."
  - title: "Finish and serve"
    body: "Remove the eggs from the heat while they're still creamy and slightly softer than you'd like—they'll continue to cook from the residual heat. Transfer to a serving plate and finish generously with chopped chives."
howToServe:
  - "Serve over toasted sourdough or your favorite bread."
  - "Use in a breakfast sandwich with cheese, avocado, or tomatoes."
  - "Serve alongside roasted potatoes and fresh vegetables."
chefsNote: "The key here is patience. Keep the heat on medium-low and stir constantly rather than trying to cook the eggs quickly. 
Take them off the heat while they're still glossy and creamy for the softest texture."
