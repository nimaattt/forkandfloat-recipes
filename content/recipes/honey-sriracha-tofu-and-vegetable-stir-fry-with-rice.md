ingredients:
  - name: "Rice"
    amount: "2 cups, jasmine or Iranian rice"
  - name: "Water"
    amount: "enough to sit about a fingernail’s depth above the rice"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "½ cup, divided between the rice and frying; use as needed"
  - name: "Extra-firm tofu"
    amount: "1 block, patted dry and cubed"
  - name: "Cornstarch"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Mixed vegetables"
    amount: "2–3 cups, chopped; onion, carrot, bell pepper, cabbage and zucchini, or vegetables of your choice"
  - name: "Sriracha"
    amount: "⅓ cup"
  - name: "Honey"
    amount: "⅓ cup"
  - name: "Lemon juice"
    amount: "⅓ cup, freshly squeezed"
  - name: "Orange juice"
    amount: "⅓ cup, freshly squeezed"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "2 cloves, grated"
  - name: "Fresh ginger"
    amount: "1 tsp, grated"
  - name: "Soy sauce"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Sesame oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Sesame seeds"
    amount: "for garnish"
  - name: "Green onions"
    amount: "optional, sliced, for garnish"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the rice"
    body: "Rinse the rice well in a small saucepan, then add water until it sits about a fingernail’s depth above the rice. Add a little salt and 1 tbsp of the olive oil. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to medium-low and gently simmer for about 30 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the water has been absorbed. Adjust the water and cooking time as needed for your rice."
  - title: "Coat the tofu"
    body: "Pat the tofu dry and cut it into cubes. Place in a bowl, sprinkle over the cornstarch and toss until evenly coated."
  - title: "Fry until golden"
    body: "Heat some of the remaining olive oil in a large frying pan or wok. Add the tofu and fry, turning as needed, until golden and crisp. Remove from the pan and set aside."
  - title: "Stir-fry the vegetables"
    body: "Add the chopped vegetables to the same pan, adding a little more olive oil if needed. Stir-fry over high heat for a few minutes, until slightly tender but still with some bite. Return the tofu to the pan."
  - title: "Mix the sauce"
    body: "In a separate bowl, combine the grated garlic and ginger with the honey, sriracha, soy sauce, lemon juice, orange juice and sesame oil. Mix well."
  - title: "Bring everything together"
    body: "Pour the sauce over the tofu and vegetables and toss to coat. Simmer for about 5 minutes, until the sauce thickens slightly."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Serve with the cooked rice and finish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions, if using."
howToServe:
  - "Serve the tofu and vegetables over the rice, spooning the sauce over the top."
  - "Finish with sesame seeds and, if you like, sliced green onions."
chefsNote: "I used onion, carrot, bell pepper, cabbage and zucchini, but you can use broccoli or whatever vegetables you have on hand. Keep them a little crisp rather than cooking them until soft."
