ingredients:
  - name: "Fresh mixed herbs"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Butter"
    amount: "1/4 cup"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Walnuts"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Lemon juice"
    amount: "from 1/2 lemon"
  - name: "Radishes"
    amount: "5–6"
  - name: "Green onions"
    amount: "4"
  - name: "Basil oil"
    amount: "for serving, optional"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "for serving, optional"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Roast the vegetables"
    body: "Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Place the halved radishes and sliced green onions cut-side down on a parchment-lined baking tray. Cube the butter and scatter the cubes over the radishes and green onions and season lightly with salt. Roast for about 10 minutes until tender and lightly caramelized."
  - title: "Blanch the herbs"
    body: "Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Add the fresh herbs and blanch for less than 1 minute. Immediately transfer them to an ice bath to stop the cooking. Drain well and squeeze out any excess moisture."
  - title: "Make the herb dip"
    body: "Add the blanched herbs, walnuts, garlic powder, lemon juice, salt, and pepper to a blender. Blend until smooth and creamy. Adjust seasoning if needed."
  - title: "Plate and serve"
    body: "Spread the herb dip onto a serving plate. Top with the roasted radishes and green onions. Finish with additional salt, freshly cracked black pepper, and a drizzle of basil oil or olive oil."
howToServe:
  - "Serve with warm flatbread or sourdough"
  - "Serve as part of a mezze platter"
  - "Serve alongside grilled vegetables or barbecue"
  - "Spread on toast or sandwiches"
chefsNote: "You can use any combination of fresh herbs you have on hand.(I used parsley, mint, cilantro, tarragon, and radish leaves.) 
This recipe is a great way to use up herbs that are slightly past their prime while still creating something vibrant and delicious."
