category: "Sides"
tags:
  - "vegetarian"
  - "persian"
  - "quick"
  - "sides"
prepTime: "5 mins"
cookTime: "0"
servings: "1 small jar"
difficulty: "Easy"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1J4m6J60Bf42et9jTjKcLD0V8AgYLEZ0Z"
date: 2026-07-31T19:50:40.370Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Shallots"
    amount: "3 medium"
  - name: "Hot water (not boiling)"
    amount: "1/4 cup"
  - name: "White vinegar"
    amount: "1/4 cup"
  - name: "Red wine vinegar"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Raw beet"
    amount: "1 small, peeled"
  - name: "Sugar"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Whole black peppercorns"
    amount: "5"
  - name: "Garlic clove"
    amount: "1, optional"
steps:
  - title: "Prepare the shallots"
    body: "Using a mandoline or a sharp knife, thinly slice the shallots. Transfer them to a clean glass jar or container that fits them snugly."
  - title: "Make the pickling brine"
    body: "Add the sugar and salt to the jar. Pour in the hot water and stir gently until they dissolve."
  - title: "Add the remaining ingredients"
    body: "Pour in the white vinegar and red wine vinegar. Add the peppercorns, garlic (if using), and the peeled raw beet."
  - title: "Pickle"
    body: "Seal the jar tightly and refrigerate for at least 4 hours before using. The shallots will continue to deepen in colour and flavour after a day."
howToServe:
  - "Add to salads and grain bowls."
  - "Layer into sandwiches and burgers."
  - "Spoon over tacos or wraps."
  - "Serve alongside grilled vegetables or BBQ."
  - "Top avocado toast or hummus."
chefsNote: "The raw beet naturally gives the shallots their beautiful bright pink colour without affecting the flavour very much but you can absolutely make this pickle without them. 
They keep well in the refrigerator for up to 2 months, and the colour becomes even more vibrant after the first day. (You can remove the beet after if desired) 
Use a jar that's just large enough to keep the shallots fully submerged in the pickling liquid for the best results."
