emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Garlic scapes"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Walnuts or pine nuts"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Lemon"
    amount: "1 medium"
  - name: "Fresh basil"
    amount: "6 large leaves"
  - name: "Pasta"
    amount: "300 g"
  - name: "Parmesan cheese"
    amount: "1 cup, freshly grated"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Blanch the garlic scapes"
    body: "Bring a medium pot of salted water to a boil. Add the garlic scapes and blanch for less than 1 minute."
  - title: "Cook the pasta"
    body: "Remove the garlic scapes and immediately add the pasta to the same pot of boiling water. Cook until al dente according to package instructions."
  - title: "Shock the garlic scapes"
    body: "Transfer the garlic scapes to a bowl of ice water to stop the cooking process. Once cooled, pat them dry."
  - title: "Make the pesto"
    body: "Add the garlic scapes, walnuts or pine nuts, juice of half the lemon, olive oil, 2/3 cup of the parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper to a blender or food processor. Blend until mostly smooth. Halfway through blending, add 1/2 cup of pasta water and 5 basil leaves. Continue blending until creamy and well combined."
  - title: "Finish the pasta"
    body: "In a large pan, add the pesto. Transfer the cooked pasta directly into the pan and add another 1/2 cup of pasta water. Toss everything together until the pasta is evenly coated and the sauce becomes silky and glossy."
howToServe:
  - "Serve with extra parmesan and freshly cracked black pepper."
  - "Add vegetables for a complete meal."
  - "Add grilled chicken, salmon, or shrimp if desired."
  - "Serve with a simple green salad and crusty bread."
chefsNote: "Blanching the garlic scapes helps mellow their sharpness while preserving their bright green colour. 
he reserved pasta water is essential for creating a smooth, silky pesto sauce, don't skip it. 
This pesto also works beautifully as a spread for sandwiches, wraps, or grain bowls."
