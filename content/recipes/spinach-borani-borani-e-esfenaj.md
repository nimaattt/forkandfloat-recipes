ingredients:
  - name: "Spinach"
    amount: "700 gr"
  - name: "Garlic"
    amount: "3 cloves"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Greek yogurt or Skyr"
    amount: "4 cups"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Olive oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the spinach"
    body: "Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant."
  - title: "Wilt the spinach"
    body: "Add the chopped spinach and cook on high heat until fully wilted and all the excess moisture has evaporated. Add garlic powder, salt, and pepper. The spinach should still have a bright green colour."
  - title: "Cool the mixture"
    body: "Remove from heat and transfer to the fridge for about 1 hour until fully cooled down."
  - title: "Mix with yogurt"
    body: "Add the cooled spinach mixture to the yogurt and mix well. Taste and adjust salt if needed."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Top with freshly ground black pepper and a drizzle of olive oil before serving."
howToServe:
  - "Serve alongside Iranian dishes or BBQ meats."
  - "Serve with crackers or chips as a snack or appetizer."
  - "Serve chilled or at room temperature."
chefsNote: "Make sure the spinach is fully cooled before mixing it with the yogurt so the dip stays thick and creamy rather than watery.
Cut the cooked spinach with kitchen scissors before mixing it into the yogurt so there are no large chunks left in the dip."
