dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Portobello mushrooms"
    amount: "2 medium"
  - name: "Philadelphia cream cheese"
    amount: "½ cup"
  - name: "Mozzarella cheese"
    amount: "½ cup, grated"
  - name: "Jalapeño"
    amount: "½, finely chopped"
  - name: "Fresh mint"
    amount: "⅓ cup, finely chopped"
  - name: "Fresh cilantro"
    amount: "⅓ cup, finely chopped"
  - name: "Kalamata olives"
    amount: "10, pitted"
  - name: "Extra virgin olive oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Balsamic vinegar"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Marinate the mushrooms"
    body: "Brush both sides of the portobello mushrooms with olive oil and balsamic vinegar. Season with salt, black pepper and half of the garlic powder. Let them sit for about 10 minutes while you heat up the BBQ."
  - title: "Make the filling"
    body: "Finely chop the mint and jalapeño. In a bowl, combine the cream cheese, mint, jalapeño, remaining garlic powder, a pinch of salt, black pepper and a small drizzle of olive oil. Mix until smooth, then fold in half of the grated mozzarella."
  - title: "Cook the mushrooms"
    body: "Place the mushrooms on the BBQ cap-side down and cook over medium heat for about 10 minutes, until slightly golden and softened."
  - title: "Stuff and finish cooking"
    body: "Remove the mushrooms from the BBQ and fill them with the cheese mixture. Top with the remaining mozzarella. Return them to the BBQ, this time gill-side up, and cook over medium-high heat for another 10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and lightly golden."
  - title: "Garnish and serve"
    body: "Finely chop the cilantro and Kalamata olives. Remove the mushrooms from the BBQ and top with the cilantro, olives and an extra crack of black pepper if desired."
howToServe:
  - "Alongside grilled meats"
  - "With a fresh green salad"
  - "As a vegetarian main course"
  - "With grilled vegetables and flatbread"
chefsNote: "Portobello mushrooms release a lot of moisture as they cook, which makes them perfect for the BBQ. 
Don't skip the balsamic marinade. It adds a subtle sweetness that works beautifully with the jalapeños and herbs.
You can make these in the oven too if a BBQ is not available."
