title: "Homemade Apple & Nut Granola"
description: "A crunchy homemade granola packed with nuts and seeds."
category: "Breakfast"
tags:
  - "quick"
prepTime: "15 mins"
cookTime: "35 mins"
servings: "10-12"
difficulty: "Easy"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1Lxno0DldEoooHDSSGneJ5gmK_HDHN-_D"
date: 2026-09-16T21:29:42.528Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Rolled oats"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Hemp seeds"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Almond flour"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Chia seeds"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Apple"
    amount: "1, finely diced"
  - name: "Cinnamon"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Coconut oil"
    amount: "2/3 cup, melted"
  - name: "Vanilla extract"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Maple syrup or honey"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Butter"
    amount: "1/2 cup, melted"
  - name: "Raw almonds"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Raw cashews"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Raw walnuts or pecans"
    amount: "2/3 cup"
  - name: "Unsweetened coconut shreds"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Dried cranberries"
    amount: "2/3 cup"
  - name: "Raw pumpkin seeds"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
steps:
  - title: "Combine the dry ingredients"
    body: "In a large bowl, combine the rolled oats, hemp seeds, chia seeds, almond flour, and cinnamon. Add the finely diced apple and mix."
  - title: "Add the wet ingredients"
    body: "Pour in the melted coconut oil, maple syrup or honey, vanilla, and melted butter. Fold everything together until well combined."
  - title: "Prepare the nuts"
    body: "Add the cashews, almonds, and walnuts or pecans to a food processor. Pulse until roughly chopped, keeping some larger pieces for extra texture."
  - title: "Combine"
    body: "Add the chopped nuts to the oat mixture along with the pumpkin seeds, coconut shreds, and dried cranberries. Mix thoroughly until everything is evenly coated with the oils and sweetener."
  - title: "Prepare for baking"
    body: "Spread the granola onto a lined baking sheet in an even layer. Press the mixture down firmly so the ingredients are packed closely together."
  - title: "Bake"
    body: "Bake at 350°F for about 35 minutes, gently mixing halfway through for even baking."
  - title: "Cool"
    body: "Remove from the oven and allow the granola to cool completely on the baking sheet before breaking it into pieces. It will become crunchier as it cools."
howToServe:
  - "Serve with Greek yogurt and fresh fruit."
  - "Add milk and enjoy it like cereal."
  - "Make a granola parfait with yogurt and berries."
  - "Serve with your favorite nut butter."
  - "Enjoy on its own as an easy snack."
chefsNote: "For chunkier granola, press the mixture firmly into the baking sheet and avoid breaking it apart until it has completely cooled. Keep the nuts fairly chunky for the best texture. 
Store in an airtight container once completely cool."
