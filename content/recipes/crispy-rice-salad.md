    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Persian cucumbers"
    amount: "2 small"
  - name: "Bell pepper"
    amount: "1 small, red or orange"
  - name: "Green onions"
    amount: "2"

  - name: "Chilli powder"

  - name: "Cayenne pepper"

    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"

  - name: "Olive oil"

    amount: "optional, for serving"
steps:
  - title: "Crisp the rice"
    body: "Spread the rice on a baking sheet. Add the smoked paprika, chilli powder, cayenne pepper, sumac, salt, black pepper, and 1 tbsp sesame oil. Mix well and spread into an even layer."
  - title: "Bake or air fry at 400°F for about 15 minutes, or until the rice is golden brown and crispy throughout. Remove from the oven and allow it to cool slightly before assembling the salad."
    body: ""
  - title: "Roast the vegetables"
    body: "Roughly cut the garlic scapes and the cauliflower florets, then place them on a baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Bake or air fry at 400°F for about 10 minutes, until slightly softened and lightly browned around the edges. The vegetables should still retain a bit of crunch."
  - title: "Prepare the fresh vegetables"
    body: "Cut the cucumbers and bell pepper into bite-sized pieces. Thinly slice the green onions."
  - title: "Make the dressing"
    body: "In a bowl, combine the tahini, remaining 1 tbsp sesame oil, olive oil, lemon juice, rice vinegar, maple syrup or honey, salt, and black pepper. Whisk until smooth and creamy. Add a splash of water if needed to loosen the dressing."
  - title: "Assemble the salad"
    body: "In a large serving bowl, combine the arugula, cucumbers, bell pepper. Add the crispy rice and roasted vegetables. Pour over the dressing and toss gently until everything is evenly coated."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Top it up with green onions and pickled shallots if desired. Serve immediately for the best texture and crunch."
howToServe:
  - "Serve as a light lunch or dinner."
  - "Pair with grilled halloumi, tofu, or salmon."
  - "Bring to picnics, BBQs, or potlucks."
  - "Top with pickled shallots for extra tang and color."
chefsNote: "Garlic scapes can be replaced with asparagus or green beans depending on the season. 
Sprouted cauliflower works beautifully here, but regular cauliflower is just as delicious. 
For the best texture, assemble the salad just before serving so the rice stays crisp."
