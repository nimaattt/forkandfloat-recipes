title: "Halim Bademjoon (Persian Eggplant & Whey Dip)"
description: "A rich Persian eggplant dip made with caramelized onions, lentils, and tangy whey (kashk)."
category: "Persian-Inspired"
tags:
  - "vegetarian"
  - "persian"
prepTime: "10 mins"
cookTime: "30 mins"
servings: "2-3"
difficulty: "Easy"
coverImage: "https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1aeKD3fW9-ox3FRKkxzcqJ_IcDqbn_o93"
date: 2026-07-30T18:02:23.070Z
emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Eggplants"
    amount: "3 large or 5 medium"
  - name: "Onions"
    amount: "3 medium"
  - name: "Lentils"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Kashk (Persian whey)"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Freshly ground black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Neutral oil"
    amount: "about 1/2 cup, for frying"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the lentils"
    body: "In a medium pot, combine the lentils with 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and cook until the lentils are soft and fully cooked. Remove from the heat and set aside."
  - title: "Prepare the eggplants"
    body: "Peel the eggplants and cut them into large strips or chunks. Sprinkle generously with salt and let them sit until they release their moisture. Pat them completely dry with paper towels."
  - title: "Fry the eggplants"
    body: "Heat about 1/2 cup of neutral oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Fry the eggplants on all sides until deeply golden and tender, turning every 7–8 minutes for even browning. Remove and set aside."
  - title: "Caramelize the onions"
    body: "Thinly slice the onions and cook them over medium heat in a little oil until deeply golden, soft, and caramelized (about 20-25 mins). Reserve about 1/3 cup for garnish."
  - title: "Mash everything together"
    body: "Return the pot with the cooked lentils to medium-low heat. Add the fried eggplants and the caramelized onions (except the reserved garnish). Mash everything together until smooth and well combined."
  - title: "Add the Kashk (whey)"
    body: "Mix the Kashk with a little hot water to thin it slightly. Stir about three-quarters of the diluted kashk into the eggplant mixture and continue mashing until creamy and fully incorporated. Season with salt and black pepper to taste."
  - title: "Plate and garnish"
    body: "Transfer the Halim Bademjan to a serving dish. Top with the reserved fried onions, drizzle over the remaining Kashk, and garnish with fried dried mint (na'na dagh), if using."
howToServe:
  - "Serve warm with fresh flatbread."
  - "Enjoy as part of a mezze spread."
  - "Serve alongside grilled meats or BBQ."
  - "Pair with fresh herbs, radishes, and walnuts for a traditional Persian spread."
chefsNote: "Kashk gives Halim Bademjan its signature tangy flavor and creamy finish. 
If you can't find Persian kashk, you can substitute plain whey or thick plain yogurt, though the flavor will be milder. 
Fried dried mint (na'na dagh) is optional but highly recommended for an authentic finishing touch. 
Like many Persian dishes, Halim Bademjan tastes even better after the flavors have had a little time to meld together.
The original recipe calls for ground beef in the mixture too- add in if desired."
