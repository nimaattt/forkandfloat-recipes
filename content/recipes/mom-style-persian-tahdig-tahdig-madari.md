emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Water"
    amount: "1 cup"
  - name: "Egg"
    amount: "1 large"
  - name: "Bloomed saffron"
    amount: "2 tsp"
  - name: "All-purpose flour"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "optional, to taste"
  - name: "Neutral oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Half-cooked Persian rice"
    amount: "enough for a medium pot"
steps:
  - title: "Bloom the saffron"
    body: "Grind the saffron and bloom it with hot water or ice. Let it sit for at least 5 minutes until fully bloomed."
  - title: "Make the batter"
    body: "In a medium bowl, add the water and egg and whisk together. Add the bloomed saffron, salt, pepper, and flour. Whisk until completely smooth with no lumps remaining."
  - title: "Heat the pot"
    body: "Place a medium pot over medium heat and let it get fully hot before adding the oil. Add the oil and wait another 2–3 minutes until the oil is hot."
  - title: "Cook the tahdig base"
    body: "Pour the batter into the pot and spread evenly. Cook on medium heat with the lid on until the surface is slightly set (3,4 mins) and touching it with your fingertip does not leave a trace."
  - title: "Add the rice"
    body: "Add the half-cooked rice on top of the tahdig layer and shape gently. Cover with the lid and cook for about 20 minutes until the rice is fully cooked and the bottom is crispy and golden."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Flip the rice onto a large flat serving plate and serve immediately."
howToServe:
  - "Serve alongside stews, kabobs, or grilled vegetables."
  - "Best served fresh and hot."
  - "Can be served as part of a traditional Persian meal spread."
chefsNote: "Make sure the pot is fully hot before adding the oil and batter, otherwise the tahdig may stick and won’t flip properly.

This is not ideal for meal prep, but you can prepare the batter ahead of time and keep it in the fridge for up to 1 day before cooking."
