ingredients:
  - name: "Eggplants"
    amount: "2 large or 4 medium"
  - name: "Greek yogurt"
    amount: "3 cups"
  - name: "Garlic cloves"
    amount: "2, minced, optional"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "2 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Grill the eggplant"
    body: "Grill the eggplants over charcoal for the best smoky flavour. If that's not available, cook them on a gas BBQ until they are completely soft. The skins should become charred, shriveled, and begin to bubble as the flesh cooks through."
  - title: "Cool and peel"
    body: "Remove the eggplants from the heat and let them cool for about 30 minutes. Once cool enough to handle, peel away the charred skin."
  - title: "Prepare the eggplant"
    body: "Finely chop the eggplant until it reaches an almost paste-like consistency."
  - title: "Mix the \"borani\""
    body: "Transfer the chopped eggplant to a bowl. Add the Greek yogurt, minced garlic (or garlic powder), salt, and pepper. Mix well until everything is evenly combined."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Transfer to a serving bowl and finish with a few grinds of black pepper before serving."
howToServe:
  - "Alongside Persian BBQ or grilled vegetables"
  - "With warm flatbread"
  - "As part of a mezze or appetizer spread"
  - "With fresh herbs and walnuts"
chefsNote: "Cooking the eggplants over charcoal makes all the difference and gives Borani Bademjan its signature smoky flavour. 
If using fresh garlic, let the dip sit in the fridge for 30 minutes before serving so the flavours have time to develop"
