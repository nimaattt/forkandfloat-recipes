ingredients:
  - name: "Riccioli pasta or desired pasta shape"
    amount: "500 g, dry"
  - name: "Spring peas"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Sugar snap peas"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Arugula"
    amount: "2–3 cups"
  - name: "Lemon juice"
    amount: "juice of 2 large lemons"
  - name: "Extra virgin olive oil"
    amount: "1/2 cup"
  - name: "Dijon mustard"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Truffle oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Parmesan cheese"
    amount: "1 cup, grated"
  - name: "Garlic powder"
    amount: "1 tsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
  - name: "Black pepper"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Cook pasta"
    body: "Bring a medium pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until al dente. Strain and rinse with cold water to stop the cooking process."
  - title: "Blanch the vegetables"
    body: "Slice the sugar snap peas diagonally. Add them to the same boiling water along with the spring peas and cook for about 1 minute. Strain and immediately transfer to a bowl of ice-cold water to stop the cooking and preserve the bright green color."
  - title: "Make the dressing"
    body: "In a bowl, whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, Dijon mustard, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and grated parmesan cheese. Once fully mixed, stir in the truffle oil."
  - title: "Mix everything together"
    body: "Add the dressing to a large serving bowl. Add the pasta, blanched vegetables, and arugula. Toss everything together until fully coated. Finish with freshly grated parmesan and freshly ground black pepper on top."
howToServe:
  - "Serve as a side for BBQ dishes."
  - "Serve on its own for a picnic lunch."
  - "Best served slightly chilled or at room temperature."
chefsNote: "Can be made ahead of time and mixed just before serving. 
Great for meal prep but keep the dressing separate and toss everything together before serving for the freshest texture."
