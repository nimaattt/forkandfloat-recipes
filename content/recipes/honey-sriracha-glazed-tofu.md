emoji: "🍽️"
dietaryTags: ""
ingredients:
  - name: "Extra-firm tofu"
    amount: "1 block"
  - name: "Cornstarch"
    amount: "1/3 cup"
  - name: "Honey"
    amount: "1/3 cup"
  - name: "Sriracha"
    amount: "1/3 cup"
  - name: "Soy sauce"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Sesame oil"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "Neutral oil"
    amount: "2 tbsp, for frying"
  - name: "Sesame seeds"
    amount: "for garnish"
  - name: "Green onions"
    amount: "for garnish, sliced"
steps:
  - title: "Press the tofu"
    body: "Wrap the tofu in a clean kitchen towel and place something heavy on top for a few minutes to remove as much moisture as possible."
  - title: "Coat the tofu"
    body: "Cut the tofu into your desired size (cubes work best). Toss with the cornstarch until every piece is evenly coated."
  - title: "Cook the tofu"
    body: "Heat a generous drizzle of neutral oil in a large pan over medium-high heat."
  - title: "Add the tofu in a single layer and cook for about 10 minutes, turning only after each side has become golden brown and crispy. This helps develop the best texture."
    body: ""
  - title: "Make the glaze"
    body: "In a separate pan, combine the honey, sriracha, and soy sauce. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring until smooth and well combined."
  - title: "Finish the dish"
    body: "Stir in the sesame oil, then add the crispy tofu. Toss gently for 1–2 minutes until every piece is coated in the glaze."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Transfer to a serving plate and garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions."
howToServe:
  - "Serve over steamed rice."
  - "Fill crispy rice paper and seaweed cups (see recipe in the recipe hub)."
  - "Pair with steamed broccoli, bok choy, or roasted vegetables."
  - "Serve with extra sriracha for additional heat."
chefsNote: "The key to crispy tofu is removing as much moisture as possible before coating it with cornstarch. 
Be patient when frying, let each side become deeply golden before turning. 
This recipe is best enjoyed immediately while the tofu is still crispy, but leftovers can be reheated in the air fryer to bring back some of the crunch."
