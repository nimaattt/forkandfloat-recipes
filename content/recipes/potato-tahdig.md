ingredients:
  - name: "Iranian rice or Jasmine rice"
    amount: "2 cups"
  - name: "Potatoes"
    amount: "2 medium"
  - name: "Bloomed saffron"
    amount: "2 tsp"
  - name: "Neutral oil"
    amount: "2 tbsp"
  - name: "Salt"
    amount: "to taste"
steps:
  - title: "Cook the rice"
    body: "Wash the rice until the water runs clear. Transfer it to a medium pot and add enough water to cover the rice with about two knuckles of water above it. Add 1 tbsp salt and 1 tbsp oil. Bring to a boil over high heat and cook for 6–7 minutes, until the rice is partially cooked but still firm in the centre. Drain the rice and set aside."
  - title: "Prepare the potatoes"
    body: "While the rice cooks, peel the potatoes and cut them into fry-sized pieces. Place them in a bowl of salted water for a few minutes, then drain and pat them completely dry. Pour the bloomed saffron over the potatoes and toss until evenly coated."
  - title: "Make the tahdig"
    body: "Heat a medium pot over high heat. Once hot, add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. When the oil is hot, arrange the potatoes in a single layer and let them fry for about 3 minutes. Gently spoon the drained rice over the potatoes without disturbing the layer underneath. Reduce the heat to medium, cover with the lid, and cook for about 20 minutes until the potatoes are golden and crispy."
  - title: "Serve"
    body: "Remove the pot from the heat and let it rest for 3–4 minutes. Carefully flip the rice onto a serving platter to reveal the crispy potato tahdig, or serve directly from the pot with a spatula."
howToServe:
  - "Alongside stews"
  - "With grilled kabobs or roasted vegetables"
  - "With yogurt and fresh herbs"
chefsNote: "Make sure both the pot and the oil are fully hot before adding the potatoes, this helps create a crispy crust that releases easily when flipped. 
Don't overcrowd the potato layer, as a single even layer creates the best tahdig."
